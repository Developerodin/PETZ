3.1 Site-wide: header, menu, footer, 404
SW-1 Mobile header hides the main action and scrolls away
 HIGH
LOCATION
Header, all pages, phone and tablet (below 992px)
STEPS
Open any page at 375px or 768px wide. Look at the header, then scroll.
EXPECTED
The primary action, Assess My Pet, is visible in the header and stays reachable while

ACTUAL

Header links measured at 375px and 768px: PETZ, Sign in, menu button. Computed header

 MEDIUM
LOCATION
Header menu, phone and tablet
